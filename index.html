About me

My academic affiliation is with the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, where my research interests align with the tiny Machine Learning and Embedded Computing (tML-EC) Lab.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and computing systems, with a particular focus on large language models (LLMs), AI accelerators, efficient AI systems, and agentic AI. I am particularly interested in bridging advances in machine learning with hardware–software co-design to develop faster, more energy-efficient, and resource-efficient AI systems.

Previously, I worked as an AI researcher at TÜBİTAK BİLGEM and the COSMOS AI Research Group at Yıldız Technical University, where I contributed to the development of large-scale Turkish language models. My research experience encompasses corpus curation, synthetic data generation, large-scale pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, preference alignment, multimodal learning, and model deployment. I also gained industry experience at CBOT, developing efficient language models for natural language processing applications.

I received my BSc in Computer Engineering from Yıldız Technical University and studied Computer Science at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) as an Erasmus exchange student.
